Our Story

A decade of perfecting the perfect cup

Founded in 2015 by two coffee-obsessed friends, Brew & Bean started as a tiny corner shop with one mission: to serve coffee that makes people pause, smile, and savor.

Today, we source beans from over 12 countries, roast them in-house weekly, and serve them with the same care we had on day one. Every cup is a conversation between bean, brewer, and you.
