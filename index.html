Our Heritage

Three generations of coffee mastery

Founded in the cobblestone heart of the old quarter, Maison Noir began as a single roaster's dream. Today, we remain faithful to that original vision — sourcing the world's finest beans and treating each cup as an act of craft.

Our master roasters travel to Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra each harvest, selecting only the top 1% of each crop. The result is a cup that speaks of place, season, and patience.
