Our Story · Est. 1987

The Art of Travel, Perfected

For nearly four decades, Aurelia has redefined what it means to journey — not as tourists, but as guests of the world's most extraordinary places and people.

Our Beginning

A Legacy Born of Wanderlust

In the spring of 1987, in a small Mayfair townhouse, Eleanor Aurelia Whitmore opened the doors to what would become one of the world's most revered travel houses. A former diplomat's daughter with a passport worn soft from use, she believed travel should be more than movement — it should be metamorphosis.

What began as a private consultancy for friends and family soon attracted a clientele who sought something the guidebooks could not offer: access, intuition, and an almost ancestral knowledge of the world's hidden corners.

Today, under the stewardship of her granddaughter, Isolde, Aurelia remains a family affair — still operating from the same Mayfair address, still answering every call personally, still believing that the finest journeys are those that change you.

— The Aurelia Family
Our Philosophy

Three Tenets

The principles upon which every Aurelia journey is built.

I

Depth Over Distance

We do not collect countries. We cultivate understanding. A single week in Kyoto, properly guided, is worth a lifetime of passing through.

II

People Over Places

The soul of any destination is its people. We connect our guests with artisans, historians, chefs, and storytellers — not attractions.

III

Legacy Over Luxury

True luxury is not what you consume — it is what you leave behind. We partner only with those who protect the places they share.
