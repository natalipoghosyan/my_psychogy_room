Compassionate Care for Your Mental Well-being

Hello, I'm Natali Poghosyan — a licensed clinical psychologist with over a decade of experience helping individuals overcome anxiety, depression, trauma, and relationship challenges.

I believe that everyone deserves to live a fulfilling, balanced life. My approach combines evidence-based therapeutic techniques with genuine empathy, creating a safe, non-judgmental space where you can explore your thoughts, feelings, and experiences at your own pace.

I hold a Ph.D. in Clinical Psychology and am certified in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR, and Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR).

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about therapy and my practice.

How do I know if therapy is right for me? ▼

If you're experiencing persistent feelings of sadness, anxiety, or overwhelm, or if you're facing a difficult life transition, therapy can be incredibly beneficial. I offer a free 15-minute consultation to help you determine if therapy is the right step for you.

How long does therapy typically take? ▼

The duration varies depending on your goals and the complexity of your situation. Some clients see significant improvement in 8-12 sessions, while others benefit from longer-term therapy. We'll regularly assess your progress together.

Do you offer online therapy sessions? ▼

Yes! I offer secure video sessions that are just as effective as in-person appointments. Online therapy provides flexibility and convenience while maintaining the same high quality of care.

What is your cancellation policy? ▼

I require at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a fee. I understand that emergencies happen and handle each situation with flexibility and understanding.

Is everything I share confidential? ▼

Absolutely. Confidentiality is the foundation of our therapeutic relationship. Everything discussed in sessions is strictly confidential, with very few legally mandated exceptions which I'll explain during our first session.
