Our Story

Crafted with Passion,
Served with Soul

At Aurum & Brew, we believe coffee is more than a beverage—it's a ritual. We source our beans directly from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, roasting each batch in small quantities to preserve the unique character of every origin.

Our baristas are artisans, trained in the delicate craft of extraction, ensuring every pour is a perfect balance of flavor, aroma, and warmth.
